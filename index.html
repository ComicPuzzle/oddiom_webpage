How it works

  1. Learn

    Work through decks of phrases one card at a time. Front is the phrase, back is what it actually means and when people use it. Cards you keep missing come back around.

  2. Submit

    Heard something good? Made something up? Add it. Every phrase in Oddiom was written by someone who thought it was worth passing on.

  3. Rate

    Readers score each phrase. Good ones rise, filler sinks, and the best submitters end up on the leaderboard with their names next to their work.

The leaderboard

Every rating a phrase receives counts towards the person who submitted it. Contributors are ranked by the average score of their phrases, with a minimum number of ratings before anyone can chart — so one lucky card doesn't put you at the top. Rankings are public and update continuously.

Questions

Does it cost anything?
No. Submitting, learning and rating are all free.
Who owns the phrases I submit?
You keep what you wrote. You grant Oddiom permission to display it in the app and credit it to your username.
What stops it filling up with nonsense?
Ratings, mostly. Low-scoring cards stop being shown, and anything abusive or plagiarised can be reported and removed.
Can I use it to learn English?
Plenty of people do — idioms and slang are exactly the part that textbooks skip. Cards are tagged by region and register so you can tell a safe one from a risky one.
